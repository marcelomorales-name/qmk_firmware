Holding Lower and Raise together activates Adjust — standard QMK tri‑layer behaviour via update_tri_layer_state.

Numeric is standalone

Numeric is entered and left with TO(), not held. layer_state_set_user excludes it from the tri‑layer check so it never gets clobbered by Lower+Raise.

Dual‑role keys

Esc/Gui, Quote/Gui, and Z/Slash with Ctrl are mod‑taps. The right thumb key is a tap‑dance: tap for Space, double‑tap for Enter.
